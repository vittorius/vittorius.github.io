Hello there. This is the first post on this blog where I plan to share my
software engineering experiences, learnings and, probably, just some random
thoughts.

<!-- more -->

I'm starting this blog at pretty turbulent times. Code is written by agents, the
joy of the craft seems to be decaying, many of us face the hard burden to
reassemble themselves and give the answer to the question: "Who am I and what am
I truly willing to do?". Or, put another way, "What matters most to me now?"

This is the kind of journey I invite you to embark on together with me. Hold
fast, and we're going to make it.
